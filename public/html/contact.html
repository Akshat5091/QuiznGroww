How does the leaderboard work?

When you sign in, every quiz attempt records your score, accuracy, and streak. Top scorers with the highest accuracy rank on the Global Hall of Fame.

Absolutely! You can practice all 18 topics anonymously. Creating an account simply enables cross-device streak tracking and leaderboard rankings.
