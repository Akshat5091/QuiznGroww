⚖️ Quiz Scoring & Difficulty Guide

Master how points, penalties, and speed multipliers work.

🌱
Normal / Easy Level +10 Correct • 0 Penalty

Zero points deducted for mistakes. Every correct answer awards +10 XP. Recommended for exploring new topics, casual study, and stress-free learning.

Medium Level +10 Correct • -5 Penalty

Moderate stakes. Correct answers earn +10 XP, while wrong answers deduct -5 XP (50% penalty). Tests your accuracy, precision, and true conceptual depth.

🔥
Hard / Difficult Level +10 Correct • -10 Penalty

Tournament-grade stakes with full 100% negative marking. Correct answers award +10 XP, but every wrong answer costs -10 XP, completely cancelling out a correct question. For true subject masters!
